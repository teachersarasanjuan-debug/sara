COMMON QUESTIONS

Business English training: frequently asked questions.

Can you train professionals and teams anywhere in Spain?

Yes. Sessions are delivered live online and can be arranged for professionals and small teams anywhere in Spain.

Can the lessons be tailored to our sector and job roles?

Yes. Examples, vocabulary and activities are built around the work situations and communication tasks your people need to practise.

What are the starting prices?

Individual coaching starts at €35 per 60-minute session. Teams of 2–4 start at €45 per 60-minute group session, and teams of 5–6 start at €50 per 60-minute group session. A 12-week programme is quoted individually. Team prices are per group, not per person; the final proposal is confirmed in writing.

Can our company receive progress updates?

A periodic summary can be agreed with the company. It may cover attendance, work situations practised, objectives and observable progress; the format is agreed before training begins.

Do we need to know the exact team size and English level before enquiring?

No. An estimate is enough for the first conversation. The format and goals can be clarified before a written proposal is prepared.
