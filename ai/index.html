THE TEACHER SARA METHOD

Not just more practice.
Better practice.

The aim is not to keep a learner chatting for longer. It is to help them notice something, use it correctly and carry it into a new situation.

01

Set a clear objective

Every activity focuses on a skill the learner can understand and practise.

02

Give useful feedback

Show the learner's words, a better version and a simple explanation.

03

Try again in context

Repeat the skill in a new sentence or situation instead of just reading a correction.

04

Keep the teacher involved

Teacher review and guidance remain part of the learning journey.
